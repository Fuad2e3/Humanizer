What the score means

It measures patterns. Stock phrases, sentences of the same length, missing contractions, filler openers and repeated words. Lower means fewer of them.

It is not a detector. GPTZero, Turnitin and others use their own methods and can score the same text differently. No tool can promise what they will say.
