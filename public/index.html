Proshno o uttor

Eta ki AI detector ke 100% bypass kore?

Na, kono tool-i eta guarantee dite pare na, ar AI detector gulo nijeo prai-i bhul kore. Manush Lekha er kaj lekhake natural ar porte bhalo kora. Lekha koto ta AI er moto lage, sheta bujhte AI score ta ekta estimate hishebe dekhay.

Amar lekha kothay jay? Save kora hoy?

Rewrite korar jonno lekha ta Claude (Anthropic) er kache pathano hoy. History shudhu apnar nijer browser e save thake, amader kono server e na. "History" theke je kono shomoy muchhe felte paren.

Kon kon bhasha chole?

বাংলা, English ar Banglish. Input ek bhashay diye output arek bhashay-o nite paren, jemon Banglish e likhe English e.

Mode gulor parthokko ki?

Halka shudhu robot er moto jayga gulo thik kore. Shadharon sentence notun kore likhe, kintu paragraph er gothon same rakhe. Gobhir puro lekha notun kore sajay, tobe kono idea bad dey na.

Word file kivabe dibo?

"Upload" e click korun, ba .docx, .txt ba .md file editor er upor tene niye ashun. Purono .doc file hole Word e khule .docx hishebe save kore nin.

School ba office e use kora ki thik?

Apnar protishthan er niyom dekhe nin. Onek jaygay AI use korle sheta janate hoy. Manush Lekha apnar lekhake shundor korte shahajjo kore, kintu niyom mene chola apnar daitto.
