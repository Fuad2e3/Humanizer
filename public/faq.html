Frequently asked questions

Can't find your answer? Try Humanizer with your own text; it's free.

What does Humanizer do?

It rewrites text, often AI-generated, so it reads like a person wrote it. It keeps your meaning, numbers and names, and shows a report after every rewrite.

Will it get my text past AI detectors?

No tool can guarantee that, and AI detectors often get it wrong in both directions. Humanizer focuses on making your writing read naturally. The AI score it shows is an estimate based on writing patterns, not the result of a specific detector.

How is the AI score calculated?

It looks at patterns common in AI-written text: stock phrases such as "plays a vital role", sentences that are all about the same length, sentences that start with words like "Moreover", words that repeat a lot, and a lack of contractions. It needs at least 40 words. If a rewrite still scores above 45, Humanizer rewrites it once more and keeps the version with the lower score.

How does the meaning check work?

After each rewrite, Claude compares the new text with your original, gives a score out of 100, and lists any ideas that were lost or added. Numbers and locked words are also checked one by one.

What is the difference between the modes?

Light fixes only the parts that sound robotic. Balanced rewrites sentences but keeps the structure. Deep restructures the whole text while keeping every idea.

Can I change just one sentence?

Yes. Click any sentence in the result to get three new wordings that fit the paragraph, then pick the one you like.

What files can I upload?

Word (.docx), plain text (.txt) and Markdown (.md) files up to 5 MB. Older .doc files need to be saved as .docx first.

Where does my text go? Is it stored?

Your text is sent to Anthropic's Claude to be rewritten and checked. Your history is saved only in your own browser, and you can delete it at any time from History.

How many words can I rewrite?

Up to 500 words per rewrite on the free plan. Split longer documents into parts.

Is it OK to use for school or work?

Check the rules where you study or work. Many places ask you to disclose AI use. Humanizer helps you improve your writing; following those rules is up to you.
