Privacy Policy

Your text stays on your device.

Last updated 12 November 2026

The short version

Quick mode rewrites your text inside your own browser. Your text is not sent to us or to anyone else, and we don't need an account, a name or an email address to let you use it.

What stays on your device

  • The text you paste or type and every rewrite of it. The rules run in your browser.
  • Files you upload (.docx, .pdf, .txt, .md). They are read in your browser and never uploaded.
  • Your history of the last 30 rewrites, kept in your browser's local storage. Clear it any time with "Clear all" in History, or by clearing this site's data in your browser.

What the hosting service sees

Like any website, the server that delivers these pages receives standard request details such as your IP address, browser type and the page you asked for. The site is currently hosted on GitHub Pages, which handles that data under GitHub's own privacy statement. We don't receive your text this way, because Quick mode never sends it.

The fonts are served from this site, so no font service sees your visit.

Cookies and tracking

We set no cookies and run no advertising or tracking scripts. If we add visitor statistics later, we'll use a tool that needs no cookies and stores no personal data, and we'll update this page first.

AI mode

AI mode is not offered on this site today. If it is added later, text you choose to rewrite with it will be sent to an AI provider to produce the rewrite, and this page will name the provider and what it keeps before the feature goes live.

Children

Humanizer collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Questions and changes

Ask questions by opening an issue at github.com/fuad2e3/Humanizer/issues. When this policy changes, the date at the top changes too.
