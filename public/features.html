An AI humanizer that keeps your meaning

Most rewriters change words and hope for the best. Humanizer rewrites your text so it reads naturally, then checks that the facts, numbers and names you wrote are still there.

Try it free

AI pattern score, before and after

See how AI-like your text reads and what gives it away: stock phrases, flat rhythm, filler openers. A guide from writing patterns, not a detector.

Free, instant and private

Quick mode runs in your browser: no account, no key, no waiting. Your text never leaves your device.

Numbers and names stay put

Numbers, brand names, people, links and quotes are found automatically and never reworded. The report shows each one that was kept.

Reword a single sentence

Click any sentence in the result to get up to three new wordings, and keep the one you like.

Three modes, five tones

Light swaps stock phrases. Balanced also drops filler openers and adds contractions. Deep also varies sentence length.

Up to three versions at once

Get one, two or three different rewrites in a single click, each with its own score, and keep the one you like.

Word and PDF, in and out

Upload .docx, .pdf, .txt or .md files, or drag them in. Save any version as a .docx for Word or Google Docs.

English and Bangla

Quick mode knows stock phrases in English and Bangla, and replaces them with plainer wording in the same language.

History in your browser

Your last 30 rewrites are saved on your device so you can reopen, copy or delete them later.

How it works

  1. Paste or upload your text. Names and numbers are protected on their own.
  2. Pick a mode and tone, then press Humanize. Ask for up to three versions.
  3. Check the report. Reword single sentences until it reads the way you want.
