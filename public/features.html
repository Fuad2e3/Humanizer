An AI humanizer that keeps your meaning

Most rewriters change words and hope for the best. Humanizer rewrites your text so it reads naturally, then checks that the facts, numbers and names you wrote are still there.

Try it free

AI score, before and after

See how AI-like your text reads before and after the rewrite, and which patterns give it away: stock phrases, flat sentence lengths, repeated openers. If a rewrite still scores high, it is rewritten once more automatically and the better version is kept.

Meaning check on every rewrite

Claude compares each rewrite with your original, scores how much meaning was kept, and lists anything that was lost or added.

Numbers and names stay put

Every number in your text is checked automatically. Lock brand names, people and technical terms so they are never reworded.

Reword a single sentence

Click any sentence in the result to get three new wordings that fit the paragraph. Keep the rest exactly as it is.

Three modes, five tones

Light fixes only the robotic parts, Balanced rewrites sentences, Deep restructures the whole text. Pick Natural, Casual, Formal, Academic or Blog.

Up to three versions at once

Get one, two or three different rewrites in a single click, each with its own score, and keep the one you like.

Word files welcome

Upload .docx, .txt or .md files, or drag them onto the editor. Files are read in your browser.

History in your browser

Your last 30 rewrites are saved on your device so you can reopen, copy or delete them later.

How it works

  1. Paste or upload your text. Lock any words that must not change.
  2. Pick a mode and tone, then press Humanize. Ask for up to three versions.
  3. Check the report. Reword single sentences until it reads the way you want.
