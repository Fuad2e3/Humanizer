Pricing questions

Is the Free plan really free?

Yes. Quick mode runs in your browser, so it costs nothing to run. There is no account, no card and no trial period.

When will Pro and Team be ready?

They are being built now. Until then, the Free plan has the full Quick engine, up to 3,000 words at a time.

Do I need an account?

Not for the Free plan. Your history is kept in your own browser. Pro will add an account so your history follows you to other devices.
